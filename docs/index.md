### 🌟 Latest Recommendation (2026-09-03)
**Sergei Bortkiewicz** — *Piano Concerto No. 1 in B-flat Major, Op. 16*

> Sergei Bortkiewicz's First Piano Concerto is a hidden gem of late Romanticism, overflowing with lush melodies and dramatic virtuosity reminiscent of Rachmaninoff. Listeners should pay special attention to the passionate opening movement, where sweeping orchestral themes seamlessly intertwine with dazzling keyboard passages.
