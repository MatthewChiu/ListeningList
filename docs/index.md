### 🌟 Latest Recommendation (2026-09-07)
**Alexander Scriabin** — *Piano Concerto in F-sharp minor, Op. 20*

> If you enjoy the lush Romantic piano repertoire of Lyapunov and Bortkiewicz, Scriabin's early masterpiece offers deeply poetic themes and exquisite Chopin-esque delicate fireworks. Pay special attention to the second movement's serene theme and variations, which showcase Scriabin's signature dreamy harmonic colors.
